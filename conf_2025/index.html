1st Lausanne PhD Macroeconomics Conference

December 4–5, 2025 · Lausanne, Switzerland

About

The conference took place on December 4–5, 2025, in Lausanne. The conference featured 12 PhD presentations, each followed by a discussion, covering a wide range of topics in macroeconomics. We had the opportunity to host two keynote speakers: Professor Matteo Maggiori and Carlos Thomas.
